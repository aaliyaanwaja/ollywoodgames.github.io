One season at a time

Change 10. With 60 fixtures the home screen is a scroll of 60 near-identical ticket stubs and 52 dotted links. Locked tickets look like disabled form fields, and the story's eight seasons are only headings in the list.

  1. 1
    Finished seasons fold into one card with a result stamp written by the story (for example "Survived" for season 1; the campaign team would supply one word per season). Tap to unfold and replay any fixture or chase stars.
  2. 2
    The current season is the page: its name, the competition, and a progress strip of eight boxes, filled as fixtures are won.
  3. 3
    The next fixture is the hero ticket, with the match situation (minute and score) that makes it feel like a moment in a season rather than a level number.
  4. 4
    The rest of the season are small stubs, two to a row. No dotted path between them: order is already in the numbers.
  5. 5
    Locked seasons are one dashed card each with the condition to open them; the far future is one line. The screen fits without scrolling on a 390x844 phone; the "Price: nothing" joke stays on the cover only.
