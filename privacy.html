Privacy policy: One-Man Team

Draft. This describes build 0.5.0. The owner must review it, add a contact address, and check the statistics wording against UK GDPR, EU GDPR and POPIA before the statistics service is switched on.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

The short version

No account, no advertising, no third-party trackers, no identifiers. The game can send two small anonymous statistics messages a day, which you can switch off. In this build the statistics service is not switched on, so nothing is sent.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (which moves you have completed, your stars and best times, your Daily Move results), your settings, and a record of your attempts for the in-game play log are saved in your device's local storage so the game can remember them. In the app this is also included in your phone's own backup. Clearing the game's data, or choosing "Reset progress" in the game, removes your progress.

Anonymous play statistics

To learn whether players come back and where moves are too hard, the game can send two kinds of message to a statistics address chosen by Ollywood Games:

A day message, at most once a day, on the first time you open the game that day: the game's version; whether it is the Android app, the iPhone app or the web version; the date you first opened the game and how many days ago that was; the furthest fixture you have won; and whether you played the previous day's Daily Move.

A session summary, when you leave the game: roughly how long you played (in bands such as "3-10 minutes"); how many moves you started and won; how many takes each won move needed; how many times moves broke down, by reason (for example "offside" or "tackled"); how many practice drills you finished; whether you played the Daily Move; which help offers you were shown and took; how many results you shared; and the last screen, fixture, number of takes and outcome before you left.

These messages carry no identifier of any kind: no name, no account, no device or advertising ID, no random install number. Two messages cannot be linked to each other or to you. The statistics address stores the message only, not your IP address or anything else about the request. No third-party analytics software is used.

Switching it off. Options, Your progress, Play statistics. When it is off nothing is sent and anything waiting to be sent is never sent. In the UK and the EU it starts off, and the game asks you once, after your first fixture, whether to switch it on.

In this build no statistics address has been set, so the game collects, keeps and sends nothing, and the Android app does not even have permission to use the internet. This page will say when that changes.

The Daily Move and sharing

The first 90 Daily Moves are inside the game. The web version also fetches later ones from this site as small files, one per month; like every file on the site this is an ordinary request to GitHub Pages and sends nothing about you. If you share a result, the game hands your phone's share sheet a line of text and a link to that day's move; what happens next is up to you and the app you share with.

Network use

The web version downloads the game's own files from this site and keeps a copy so it can run offline. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when files are requested, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The game contains no content that requires an age restriction and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The statistics described above are anonymous and can be switched off.

Changes

If the game ever adds advertising, accounts or other online features, or changes what the statistics contain, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
