Fewer boxes

Changes 5 and 12. Options today is seven identical shadowed boxes and two paragraphs; pause is six full-width controls. Every row looks equally important, which is the look of a template.

  1. 1
    Group rows into a few printed lists with dashed rules between them, one shadow per group. Labels say what the group is for.
  2. 2
    Slow aim explains itself in one line inside its own row, with its own icon (today it borrows the book icon because the clock icon is missing).
  3. 3
    Two accessibility settings: Text size (Large scales all reading text and labels by 1.2 and the canvas tags and squad numbers by 1.15), and Reduce motion, which follows the phone by default and can be forced on.
  4. 4
    One weight for every row. Today "Privacy policy" is a link and renders lighter than its neighbours.
  5. 5
    Pause reminds you who you are, with the full tip, and keeps one clear primary action.
  6. 6
    Settings shrink to three icon buttons under the decision instead of three full-width rows above the fold.
