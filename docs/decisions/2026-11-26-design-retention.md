# Owner decisions on the design and retention plans, Thu 26 Nov 2026

Design plan (`docs/design/2026-11-26-design-plan.md`): build all 12 changes, with one exception:
- No half-speed finish in the goal replay. The replay runs at full speed throughout (the owner dislikes slow motion).
- Sound: free-licence recordings unless the owner later buys a pack.

Retention plan (`docs/retention/2026-11-26-retention-plan.md`), launch scope approved:
- Help when stuck: +2 s on the clock after 8 fails, slow aim offered at 12, "Move on" at 16 (one per season, no stars).
- "Move on" is free. Hints may be sold later instead.
- Daily Move with the seven-day card, plus the one-tap share link that opens the daily in a browser.
- Anonymous measurement with an off switch (privacy page and Play data form updated in the same release).

Not approved for now:
- Par, gaffer's time and best time marked on the clock bar during play.
