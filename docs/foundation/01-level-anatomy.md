# Foundations 01: anatomy of the first 20 levels

Measured on the Saturday-evening build (`/home/claude/baseline`, tag `baseline-20`, original clocks), the build the owner
rated this morning. Every number comes from running the engine, not from reading the files, unless the method says so.
Scripts are in `/home/claude/foundation-work/forensics/`; the tables are generated by `ratings/gen01.py` from their JSON.

## Read this first

- Most measures follow the bot's solution, which is one route. A person takes other routes. Where it matters I also
  measured the routes a simulated person takes (see 07).
- "Takes" and "Fun" are the owner's, from 06 (set A, played this morning on the rating build).
- Round numbers (R1, R2...) follow the order the rounds are played, decoys included, as in the owner's fail codes.

## How each measure was taken (one line each)

- **Rounds**: the level's play order; B = ball player, D = decoy (level file).
- **Def. M/Z/P/L**: count of `mark`, `zone`, `press`, `line` defenders (level file). Speed: `spd` field, 5.2 if unset.
- **Keeper**: the `gk` field; "standard" = 13-tick reaction, line keeper.
- **Clock, bot s, slack**: `time`; the bot's full replay to the goal; clock minus bot.
- **Start m**: first ball player's distance from goal centre. **Ball travel**: path length of the ball in the replay.
- **Pass length**: ball position at the kick to the reception. **To space**: the receiver ran more than 3 m while the ball
  was travelling; otherwise "feet".
- **Lane m**: closest defender on his feet to the ball at any tick of the pass's flight.
- **At receiver m**: nearest defender on his feet at the reception / at any tick of the next 0.47 s.
- **Offside margin m**: at the kick, how far the receiver is onside of (second-last opponent minus 0.6 m); "behind ball"
  means he cannot be offside.
- **Hold s**: reception to kick; f = first time (within 8 ticks).
- **Spare s**: how much longer the player can stand on the ball aiming before his own round fails, capped at 3 s
  (`dwell.js`); tac = tackled, int = intercepted.
- **Decoys needed**: does the bot's route still score if the decoy stands still and presses DONE (`dwell.js`).
- **Jitter %**: `tools/test.js --jitter`, 40 runs per round with the bot's timing, aim and power nudged.
- **Gets it / carry / shot m**: the finisher's distance from goal centre at control, metres dribbled, distance at the shot.
- **Def. could win it s**: for every defender, (distance minus his lunge range) / his speed + his patience + the 0.2 s
  plant (+ any time still on the floor); the smallest. A conservative estimate of how long the finisher has.
- **Goal open m**: from the bot's exact shooting moment, a full-power shot at each of 83 points 0.1 m apart along the goal
  line; metres that score (`finish.js`).
- **Corner open s**: the finisher stands on the ball aiming for longer; the longest wait for which at least 1 m of goal
  still scores and he still has the ball. "3+" = still open after 3 s.
- **Tolerance**: one number of the bot's route moved alone; the round, every later round and the replay must still score
  (`windows2.js`). Aim is turned about the spot where the ball is kicked (`aim3.js`); power in % of the bar; run timing
  in seconds (the early side is cut off at 2 s or at kick-off, so "-2" means "at least 2").
- **Edges of the windows**: why the move broke at each edge; "R4 intercepted" in an R2 cell means a change in round 2
  got the ball intercepted in round 4.

## What stands out

1. **Every move starts deep.** 19 of 20 start 44-55 m out (fixture 7 starts at 33 m) and the ball travels 46-86 m. The
   7-14 s clocks leave the bot 1.8-7.8 s of slack; from fixture 5 on the bot uses 56-74% of the clock.
2. **The finish is nearly identical everywhere.** The bot shoots from 9.5-14.8 m (median 10.5 m) with the keeper 2.7-5 m off
   his line (1.4 m in 12), and from that moment 2.7-5.9 m of the 8 m goal scores (median 4.75 m), usually in two corners
   about 2.4 m wide. Only 11, 14 and 15 have a defender within 2 m as he shoots (0.43 s before he could win it).
3. **The jeopardy is in the middle.** From fixture 5 on, the narrowest windows are on passes before the finish: in 14 of
   those 16 fixtures the tightest pass aim is 1-14.5 degrees and the tightest pass power 5-38% of the bar, while the
   finishing shot has 10-17 degrees and its power can fall to 3-14% of the bar before the keeper saves it.
   Under the chain test most of those passes fail by being intercepted one or two rounds LATER, not in their own round.
4. **Where the owner failed matches where the windows are narrowest.** 9: the 8's diagonal (aim 5 degrees, power 6%),
   owner P2 intercepted 3 times. 18: the 8's ball through the hole (aim 1 degree), owner P4 intercepted 4 times.
   19: the 7's run (late edge 0.03 s, early edge offside at 1.03 s), owner P5 offside 7 times. 13: the 6 and 10 in
   the middle (power 22%, timing 0.93 s), owner P3 intercepted 6 times.
5. **Two decoys do nothing on the bot's route**: 4 (The decoy, the level that teaches decoys) and 12 (Push them back)
   still score if the decoy stands still. The other six decoys are needed.
6. **The colleague's finishing-aim numbers were wrong by about three times.** `windows2.js` and `tools/windows.js` turn the
   aim about the player's start spot, which for a finisher who has carried 15 m is far from where he kicks; it reported
   2.5-6.5 degrees. Turned about the kick spot, the finish has 10-17 degrees. Pass aims change less (they are corrected
   in Table 7).
