Say it once, at the right time

Change 1. Today the same sentence sits in the HUD for the whole move, a separate chip at the bottom says "You are the 8. Move to start the clock", and on early fixtures a coach card says a third thing. Three text sources compete while both thumbs are busy.

  1. 1
    One card per player, before the clock runs, in the control bar. Who you are and what to do, in the empty left half of the bar, exactly where the left thumb is about to land. It covers nothing on the pitch, and the goal and every defender stay in view while the player plans. It folds away on the first touch (120ms slide down, a paper sound) and does not come back during the move.
  2. 2
    Nothing written on the pitch outside the tutorial fixtures. Retrying the same player shows the card again, so a stuck player can re-read it; the pause card keeps the full text too.
  3. 3
    Live coach prompts stay on the pitch, on fixtures 1-4 only, because they speak during play when the left thumb covers the bar. They are then the only words on the cloth. The "No. 8 recorded" toast moves to the HUD strip as a tick appearing on the 8 (mock 01), so it never lands on the ball at the bottom of the pitch again.
