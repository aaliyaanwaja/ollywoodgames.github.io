Let the move be the reward

Change 8. "Then watch the whole team play it" is the promise on the store listing, yet the replay plays under a sheet that covers its bottom 40%, inside a HUD that still shows the retry buttons dashed out and a clock counting down that means nothing.

  1. 1
    The HUD becomes a replay ribbon: title, fixture, and the clock row showing the finished move time, frozen. The clock is still on screen; it now tells the story of the move.
  2. 2
    One clean loop of the whole move at full height, all players, the trails of each recorded run, the goal. Broadcast touch: the final 0.4s before the ball crosses the line plays at half speed in the replay only (never in live play).
  3. 3
    Tap to skip for players in a hurry; the card arrives either way after one loop (about 5 to 9 seconds).
  4. 4
    Lead with the result, not the stats: the old score struck through, the new one large, the minute. "Take 3" is a neutral count in the corner.
  5. 5
    The next star is a target, not a lecture: "Third star at 4.8s" replaces the sentence "A move of 4.8s or quicker earns the third star. Take as many goes as you like."
